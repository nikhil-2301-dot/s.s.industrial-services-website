About S. S. Industrial Services

Committed to Safety and Purity

Based in Ludhiana, Punjab, S. S. Industrial Services is a trusted supplier of industrial chemicals and advanced chlorination solutions across India.

We specialize in high-purity Liquid Chlorine Gas, Vacuum Feed Gas Chlorinators, Automatic Vacuum Regulators, Poly Aluminium Chloride, and complete chlorinator accessories, installation, and repair services. Sourcing products from leading manufacturers such as Grasim, GACL, Bodal, Trimo, NEEL Chemical, and DCM Shriram, we ensure superior quality, safety, and reliability. Our commitment to timely delivery, technical expertise, and customer-focused service makes us a dependable partner for water treatment plants, industrial facilities, and chemical processing industries nationwide.
