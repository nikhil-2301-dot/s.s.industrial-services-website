About S. S. Industrial Services

Delivering Purity, Precision, and Uncompromising Safety Since 2021.

Who We Are

Founded in 2021 and based in Ludhiana, Punjab, S. S. Industrial Services has quickly emerged as a premier wholesale distributor and service provider. We specialize in high-purity industrial chemicals and state-of-the-art pressure regulation equipment. Driven by a commitment to excellence, we cater to a diverse clientele across India, including municipal water treatment plants, industrial manufacturing facilities, and chemical processing units.

Our Mission

Our mission is to safeguard public health and optimize industrial operations by supplying superior chlorination products and failsafe dosing systems. We are dedicated to setting the industry standard for safe handling, reliable distribution, and technical expertise in industrial gas management.

Core Expertise & Product Range

The handling of industrial gases demands absolute precision and strict adherence to safety protocols. At S. S. Industrial Services, we provide a comprehensive suite of products engineered for seamless integration and dependable performance:

  • Liquid Chlorine Gas (99.8% Purity) Supplied in high-pressure tonners for large-scale disinfection and water treatment ecosystems.
  • Advanced Chlorination Systems Featuring Vacuum Feed Gas Chlorinators and Automatic Vacuum Regulators that ensure precise dosing and prevent accidental leaks. We also supply a full range of accessories and provide expert repair services for chlorinators of any make.
  • Industrial Chemicals Supplying premium Poly Aluminium Chloride to facilitate highly efficient water purification and wastewater treatment.

Why Choose Us?

  • Failsafe Safety Standards Our chlorination systems are engineered so that valves only open under active suction, guaranteeing complete isolation of gas flow in the event of a power or equipment failure.
  • Unmatched Quality & Sourcing We distribute only the highest-grade chemicals and heavy-duty regulation equipment, sourcing directly from industry-leading manufacturers such as Grasim, Bodal, Trimo, GACL, NEEL Chemical, and DCM Shriram.
  • Client-Centric Approach From initial consultations and bulk ordering to ongoing technical support, our team delivers tailored solutions to meet the unique demands of your facility.
  • Trusted Reliability As a highly rated supplier, we are known for our prompt deliveries, transparent business practices, and strict compliance with national industrial safety regulations.
  • Nationwide Reach We are proud to offer our comprehensive products and services to clients throughout India.
